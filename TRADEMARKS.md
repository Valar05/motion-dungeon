# Motion Dungeon Project Identity Policy

This policy governs project identity. It does not reduce or replace rights granted by the software and content licenses.

“Motion Dungeon” identifies Drew Clarke's original project. No claim of trademark registration is made here.

## Allowed without separate permission

You may:

- truthfully say that your work is “based on Motion Dungeon by Drew Clarke”;
- link to the official repository;
- use the name when discussing, reviewing, or accurately identifying the original project;
- describe compatibility with Motion Dungeon, provided the description does not imply endorsement.

## Forks and modified distributions

A materially modified fork must use a distinct primary product name and must not present itself as the official Motion Dungeon release. It may use a factual subtitle such as:

> Night Forge — based on Motion Dungeon by Drew Clarke

Do not use Motion Dungeon's logo, account identity, or visual branding in a way likely to imply that Drew Clarke produced, approved, sponsors, or supports the fork.

## Permission and questions

For uses that would reasonably look official, seek written permission from Drew Clarke through the repository's public contact or issue surface.

This policy concerns names, logos, and source identity. It is not an extra restriction on copying, modifying, or distributing AGPL-licensed code or CC BY-SA-licensed material.
